## AprilTag code tables

The tag families (tag36h11, tag25h9, tag16h5, tagStandard41h12) come from [AprilRobotics/apriltag](https://github.com/AprilRobotics/apriltag). They are included as data tables in [`featurescript/AprilTag.fs`](featurescript/AprilTag.fs), and the tag patterns of the tiles in the releases are generated from them.
